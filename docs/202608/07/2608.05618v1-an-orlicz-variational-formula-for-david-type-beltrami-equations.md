## 摘要
设 $\mathcal{U} \Subset \mathbb{C}$ 固定，$Φ(s)=e^s-s-1$，$F(ν)=\fracν{2+|ν|}$，并令 $f^{F(ν)}$ 表示相应Beltrami方程的主解。恒等式 $K_{F(ν)}=1+|ν|$ 将紧支撑David系数与指数Orlicz参数联系起来。我们证明，在 $L^Φ_{\mathcal{U}}(\mathbb{C})$ 中充分高阶有限指数矩可用的开子集上，主解映射是取值于 $W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb{C})$ 的局部实 $C^{1,1}$ 映射。沿方向 $η\in L^Φ_{\mathcal{U}}(\mathbb{C})$ 的导数是方程 $\bar{\partial} V - F(ν) \partial_z V = DF_ν(η) \partial_z f^{F(ν)}$ 的主规范化解。证明使用了由基础主解拉回的方法。关键估计是逐点相消不等式 $\frac{\|DF_ν\|_{\mathrm{op}}}{1-|F(ν)|^2} \le \frac{1}{2}$，它将线性化方程转化为一个源项直接由方向的$L^Φ$-范数控制的 $\bar{\partial}$-方程。结合主退化 $L^2$-预解式与指数可积畸变的最优Jacobian正则性，这给出了均匀二次余项估计。在原点处，我们得到 $W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ 中的 $D \mathrm{Sol}_0[η]=(1/2)\mathcal{C}η$。

## Abstract
Let $\mathcal{U} \Subset \mathbb{C}$ be fixed, $Φ(s)=e^s-s-1$, $F(ν)=\fracν{2+|ν|}$, and let $f^{F(ν)}$ denote the principal solution of the corresponding Beltrami equation. The identity $K_{F(ν)}=1+|ν|$ identifies compactly supported David coefficients with exponential-Orlicz parameters. We prove that, on the open subset of $L^Φ_{\mathcal{U}}(\mathbb{C})$ where a sufficiently high finite exponential moment is available, the principal solution map is locally real $C^{1,1}$ with values in $W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb{C})$. The derivative in a direction $η\in L^Φ_{\mathcal{U}}(\mathbb{C})$ is the principally normalized solution of $\bar{\partial} V - F(ν) \partial_z V = DF_ν(η) \partial_z f^{F(ν)}$. The proof uses a pullback by the base principal solution. The key estimate is the pointwise cancellation $\frac{\|DF_ν\|_{\mathrm{op}}}{1-|F(ν)|^2} \le \frac{1}{2}$, which converts the linearized equation into a $\bar{\partial}$-equation whose source is controlled directly by the $L^Φ$-norm of the direction. Combined with the principal degenerate $L^2$-resolvent and the optimal Jacobian regularity for exponentially integrable distortion, this yields a uniform quadratic remainder estimate. At the origin one obtains $D \mathrm{Sol}_0[η]=(1/2)\mathcal{C}η$ in $W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}$.
