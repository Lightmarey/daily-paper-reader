Abstract. The Liouville problem for the three-dimensional stationary Navier–Stokes equations remains open, even for axisymmetric D-solutions. In this paper, we obtain two results based on decay in the cylindrical radial variable r = |x′|.(i). Using a new pointwise Calder´ on–Zygmund estimate adapted to cylindrical geometry, we improve the decay estimates of Carrillo–Pan–Zhang (2020, JFA) and prove 

|∇ ur | + |∇ uz | ≲ r−5/4[log(e + r)] 5/4, |ωr | + |ωz | ≲ r−9/8[log(e + r)] 9/8, r ≫ 1.

(ii). We develop a new approach to Liouville theorems that improves the axisymmetric criteria of Wang (2019, JDE) and Zhao (2019, Nonlinear Anal.). Without any symmetry assumption, we show that a D-solution is trivial if one of the following holds: (a) . sup 

> |x′|=r, z ∈R

|u(x′, z )| ≤ Cr −2/3[log(e + r)] −γ ; (b) . sup 

> |x′|=r, z ∈R

|ω(x′, z )| ≤ Cr −5/3[log(e + r)] −γ ,

for r ≥ 1, where γ > 1/3.

Afundamental open problem asks whether every three-dimensional D-solution of (1.1) must be iden-tically zero. A classical sufficient condition is u ∈ L9/2(R3); see [Gal11]. Chae established a distinct scale-invariant criterion ∆ u ∈ L6/5(R3), whereas Chae–Wolf obtained a logarithmic refinement of Galdi’s L9/2-criterion. Seregin [Ser16] introduced a criterion in BM O −1(R3), Kozono–Terasawa– Wakasugi [KTW17] considered vorticity-decay and Lorentz-space conditions, and Seregin–Wang [SW20] developed corresponding annular criteria; see also [Tsa21] and the references therein. Fur-ther criteria based on the head pressure Q = 12 |u|2 + p were obtained by Chae [Cha21], who proved triviality under relative-decay assumptions comparing u or p with Q at infinity. Bang–Yang [BY25] employed Saint–Venant estimates to derive Liouville theorems from the growth of the Ls-mean oscillation of a potential function of the velocity and from the relative decay of Q and |u|2. Chae [Cha25] subsequently established further head-pressure criteria through Osgood-type conditions, level-set arguments, and the coarea formula. Coiculescu–Yang [CY26] introduced a capsule-based approach and obtained improved conditional Liouville theorems under sufficiently slow sublinear growth assumptions on antiderivatives of the velocity, including vector potentials and line integrals. More recently, Chae [Cha26] considered D-solutions whose head pressure Q, normalized to vanish at infinity, satisfies the lower power-law bound |Q(x)| ≥ C∥Q∥L∞ |x|−α for all sufficiently large 

|x|. He proved that u ≡ 0 if |u(x)| = O(|x|−β ) for some β ≥ α

|∇ Q(x)| = O(|x|−β ) for some β ≥ 2α.Symmetry-restricted flows form a natural intermediate setting in which genuinely three-dimensional structures remain accessible to sharper analysis. For helically symmetric flows, Han–Wang–Xie [HWX26] proved that every bounded smooth solution of the steady Navier–Stokes equations in R3

Axisymmetric flows retain genuinely three-dimensional phenomena, including swirl, while their cylindrical structure permits refined pointwise estimates. In the no-swirl case uθ ≡ 0, Liouville theorems were established by Koch–Nadirashvili–Seregin– ˇSver´ ak [KNS ˇS09] and Korobkov–Pileckas– Russo [KPR15]. For axisymmetric flows with swirl, Chae–Weng [CW16a] obtained several sufficient conditions for triviality. A complementary line of research concerns the asymptotic behavior of ax-isymmetric D-solutions. Pointwise decay estimates were developed by Choe–Jin [CJ09], Weng [Wen18], and Carrillo–Pan–Zhang [CPZ20]; in particular, Carrillo–Pan–Zhang derived logarithmic decay bounds for the velocity and the vorticity components.
