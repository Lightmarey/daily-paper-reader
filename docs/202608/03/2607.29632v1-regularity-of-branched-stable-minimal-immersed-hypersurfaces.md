## 摘要
我们建立了分支稳定极小浸入超曲面的非分支奇异集的Hausdorff维数的一个尖锐界，其奇异集具有局部有限的$\mathcal H^{n-2}$-测度：当$n=2$时非分支奇异集为空，当$n=3$时为离散集，当$n\geq4$时Hausdorff维数至多为$n-3$。我们还构造了$\mathbb R^4$中一个由分支极小浸入产生的非平坦稳定极小锥，其顶点是一个非分支奇点。与欧几里得因子取乘积给出了非分支奇异集的Hausdorff维数恰好为$n-3$的例子，表明我们的正则性界在每一维$n\geq3$中都是尖锐的。我们证明中的主要工具是一个广义Schoen不等式和相应的在平稳经典锥和超平面并集附近的分支片状定理。

## Abstract
We establish a sharp bound on the Hausdorff dimension of the non-branch singular set of branched stable minimal immersed hypersurfaces whose singular sets have locally finite $\mathcal H^{n-2}$-measure: the non-branch singular set is empty when $n=2$, discrete when $n=3$, and has Hausdorff dimension at most $n-3$ when $n\geq4$. We also construct a non-flat stable minimal cone in $\mathbb R^4$ arising from a branched minimal immersion whose vertex is a non-branch singularity. Taking products with Euclidean factors yields examples whose non-branch singular sets have Hausdorff dimension exactly $n-3$, showing that our regularity bound is sharp in every dimension $n\geq3$. The main ingredients in our proof are a generalized Schoen inequality and a corresponding branched sheeting theorem near stationary classical cones and unions of hyperplanes.
