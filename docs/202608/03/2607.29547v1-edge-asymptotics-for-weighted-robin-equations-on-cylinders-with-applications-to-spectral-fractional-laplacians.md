## 摘要
对于圆柱上一类加权退化或奇异问题，采用Almgren型单调性方法推导出在底面与侧面之间的边缘附近解的渐近估计，在该边缘处施加了Robin边界条件。作为一个相关应用，获得了Robin和Neumann谱分数拉普拉斯算子的局部渐近性和从边界的唯一延拓。解的局部衰减速率对于Neumann问题和Robin问题都是相同的，并且由一个具有某种对称性的加权球面谱问题明确量化。

## Abstract
For a class of weighted degenerate or singular problems on a cylinder, Almgren-type monotonicity methods are employed to derive asymptotic estimates of solutions near the edge between the base and the lateral surface, where a Robin boundary condition is imposed. As a relevant application, local asymptotics and unique continuation from the boundary are obtained for the Robin and Neumann spectral fractional Laplacians. The local decay rates of the solutions are the same for both the Neumann and the Robin problems and are explicitly quantized by a weighted spherical spectral problem with some symmetry.
