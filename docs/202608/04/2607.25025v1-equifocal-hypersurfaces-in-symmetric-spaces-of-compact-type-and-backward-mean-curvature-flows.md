## 摘要
我们首先推导了单连通不可约紧致型对称空间中等焦超曲面的平均曲率及形状算子范数平方的公式。这些公式用等焦超曲面的切向焦点数据显式给出。第三，我们研究了等焦超曲面的后向平均曲率流。Liu和Radeschi已建立了该流对于等焦超曲面的长时间存在性。我们分析了沿长时间解的平均曲率和形状算子范数平方的时间演化，从而推广了Liu和Terng关于球面中等参超曲面的结果。我们的分析还将Liu-Terng关于球面中后向平均曲率流的猜想推广到单连通不可约紧致型对称空间。

## Abstract
We first derive a formula for the mean curvature and the squared norm of the shape operator of equifocal hypersurfaces in simply-connected irreducible symmetric spaces of compact type. The formulas are given explicitly in terms of the tangential focal data of the equifocal hypersurfaces. Third, we study the backward mean curvature flow for equifocal hypersurfaces. The long-time existence of this flow for an equifocal hypersurface was established by Liu and Radeschi. We analyze the time evolution of the mean curvature and the squared norm of the shape operator along the long-time solution, thereby we generalize the result of Liu and Terng for isoparametric hypersurfaces in the sphere. Our analysis also gives an extension of Liu-Terng conjecture on the backward mean curvature flows in the sphere to the simply-connected irreducible symmetric space of compact type.
