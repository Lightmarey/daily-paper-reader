## 摘要
我们证明了具有有限动能的表面准地转（SQG）方程存在无穷多个光滑非径向稳态解。我们的构造基于二维半 Yamabe 方程的一族非径向变号解，这些解通过 Lyapunov–Schmidt 约化获得，并集中在正多边形的顶点处。当顶点数趋于无穷时，相应的稳态 SQG 解收敛到以原点为中心的径向稳态轮廓，并带有低阶涡层修正。

## Abstract
We prove the existence of infinitely many smooth nonradial stationary solutions to the surface quasi-geostrophic (SQG) equation with finite kinetic energy. Our construction is based on a family of nonradial sign-changing solutions to the two-dimensional half-Yamabe equation, obtained via a Lyapunov--Schmidt reduction and concentrated at the vertices of a regular polygon. As the number of vertices tends to infinity, the associated stationary SQG solutions converge to a radial stationary profile centered at the origin, together with a lower-order vortex sheet correction.

## 6. 主要结论与发现

- **定理1.1**：存在无穷多个光滑非径向有限动能的 SQG 稳态解 \(\theta_k = \psi_k^3\)。并证明（子序列意义下）
  \[
  \log k \left(\theta_k + \frac{U}{3}\right) \rightharpoonup \frac{\pi}{\sqrt 2}\sigma_{\partial D} + R
  \]
  其中 \(R\) 属于 \(\dot H^s \cap L^p\)（\(p<\infty\)，\(0<s<1/2\)）。解的动能随 \(k\) 增大并不一致有界。
- **定理1.2**：对足够大的 \(k\)，半 Yamabe 方程存在解
  \[
  \psi_k(x)=\sum_{j=1}^k \frac{1}{\sqrt{\delta_k}} U\!\left(\frac{x-\xi_{k,j}}{\delta_k}\right)-U(x)+\phi_k(x)
  \]
  其中 \(\|\phi_k\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}\to0\)，且 \(\delta_k \sim 1/(k\log k)^2\)。
- 关于正泡符号的猜想修正：文献[18]猜测一般 s 情形 \(\delta_k\sim k^{-2}\)，本文证明在 n=2、s=1/2 时该猜测不成立，实际速率为 \((k\log k)^{-2}\)。
