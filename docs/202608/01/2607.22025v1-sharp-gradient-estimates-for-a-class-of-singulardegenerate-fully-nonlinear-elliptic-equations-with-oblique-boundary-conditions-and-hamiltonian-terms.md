## 摘要
本文关注一类在C^1域上带斜边界条件和哈密顿项的奇异或退化完全非线性椭圆方程。在关于模型奇异性/退化性的相当一般的条件下，我们在统一框架内建立了直到边界的尖锐C^{1,α}正则性。

## Abstract
This paper focus on a class of singular or degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equations with Hamiltonian terms under oblique boundary conditions on $C^{1}$ domains. Under quite general conditions on the singularity/degeneracy of the model, we establish the sharp $C^{1,α}$ regularity up to the boundary within a unified framework.
