Abstract 

This paper focus on a class of singular or degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equations with Hamiltonian terms under oblique boundary conditions on C1 domains. Under quite general con-ditions on the singularity/degeneracy of the model, we establish the sharp C1,α regularity up to the boundary within a unified framework. Mathematics Subject classification (2020): 35B65; 35J60; 35J70; 35J75; 35D40. Keywords: Global regularity; fully nonlinear degenerate/singular equations; oblique boundary conditions; Hamiltonian terms; viscosity solution. 

# 1 Introduction 

Since the 1980s, the notion of the viscosity solution has been applied widely in the study of non-divergent equations especially of fully nonlinear elliptic equations, and a series of important interior regularity results and global results with Dirichlet/Neumann/Oblique boundary conditions have been obtained (see [23, 24, 41, 42, 46, 47] and references therein) . Despite of the profound importance of the aforementioned works, a large number of mathematical models involve operators whose diffusion properties (ellipticity) may degenerate or blow-up along an a priori unknown region, such as S(u) := {x : Du (x) = 0 }, which depends on the solution itself. From the mathematical view point, this fact impels less efficient diffusion features for the model near such a region and therefore the regularity theory for solutions to such equations become more sophisticated. In this paper, we examine the gradient regularity of viscosity solutions to the following degener-ate/singular fully nonlinear elliptic equations with Hamiltonian terms and oblique boundary condition

We remark that the word oblique means "having a sloping direction". In (1.2), β

plays a role of slope. When β makes an angle more than some fixed level on ∂Ω, the condition (1.2) is said to be regular and otherwise it is said to be nonregular. By way of illustration, the simplest example of the regular oblique boundary condition is the Neumann boundary condition, that is, the case of β = n and γ = 0 where n is the outward normal vector of ∂Ω. Therefore, we can view the condition (1.2) as a sort of generalization of the Neumann boundary condition. The study of equations like (1.1) arises naturally in the optimal stochastic control problems [13, 27, 34, 40], in image enhancements [14, 26], and as a generalization of Hamilton-Jacobi equations [2, 38]. On the other hand, problems involving oblique tangential derivative conditions are naturally motivated by the modeling of several phenomena, such as reflected shocks in transonic flow [25], the theory of capillary problems [31, 33], Brownian motion [32, 44]. Over the last two decades, the theory of equations like (1.1) has experienced remarkable progress. In the pioneering works [5– 8], existence and uniqueness, maximum principles, Harnack inequality and Hölder regularity were obtained. The graident Hölder regularity of solutions is the subject of groundbreaking work [37], where they established interior C1,α regularity of viscosity solutions to |Du |pF (D2u) = f with p ≥ 0;the optimality of its Hölder exponent for the same problem is shown in [1]. Subsequently, this type of local regularity result has been extended to various kinds of degenerate/singular fully nonlinear equations, please refer to [4, 14, 16, 17, 29, 30, 35] and the references therein. It is noteworthy to mention that the recent paper [36] derived local optimal C1,α regularity of solutions to (1.1). As for the Dirichlet problem in the purely degenerate/singular setting, i.e., H ≡ 0, there has been much progress in the regularity of solutions. For instance, if Φ( t, x ) = tp with p ≥ 0, Araújo and Sirakov [3] obtained sharp boundary and global C1,α regularity in the presence of sufficiently regular domain and boundary datum. More or less simultaneously, Bezerra J ´unior et al. [16] considered the variable-exponent nonhomogeneous scenario, i.e., Φ( t, x ) = tp(x) + a(x)tq(x) with 0 ≤ p(·) ≤ q(·), and proved that solutions are C1,α regularity up to the boundary for some α > 0 depending additionally on the regularity of the boundary ∂Ω, as well as on the boundary data. Afterwards, these results were extended to the Dirichlet problem exhibiting more general degeneracy/singularity in [18]. On the other hand, with regard to the Dirichlet problem for the singular or degenerate fully nonlinear equations involving Hamiltonian terms, Birindelli-Demengel’s seminal works [9–12] investigated the Dirichlet problem (1.1) with Φ( t, x ) = tp, H(t, x ) = h(x)|t|m and B = u on a C2 domain. Based on an improvement-of-flatness approach, they obtained global C1,α regularity for some unspecified exponent α ∈ (0 , 1) in [9–11] for the sublinear/linear cases 0 < m ≤ p + 1 , and in [12] for the superlinear/subquadratic cases p + 1 < m ≤ p + 2 .Within the framework of Neumann boundary conditions, Patrizi [43] proved a global C1,α reg-ularity for the singular case under the homogeneous Neumann condition and on a C2 domain. Sub-sequently, Banerjee and Verma [15] considered the Neumann problem (1.1) with Φ( t, x ) = tp (p ≥ 0), 

H = 0 , β = n and γ = 0 , and established C1,α regularity up to the boundary on a C2 domain. In this setting, the optimal gradient Hölder regularity was derived in [45]. 2In the context of oblique boundary conditions, Byun et al. [19] investigated (1.1) with Φ( t, x ) = 

tp (p ≥ 0) and H = 0 . Based on perturbation and compactness arguments, they proved sharp 

C1,α estimate up to the boundary. Concerning this problem, Bessa et al. [20] extended this result for the optimal gradient regularity theory to the degenerate oblique setting under relaxed convexity assumptions. Afterwards, Byun et al. [21] studied the problem (1.1) with H = 0 and Φ exhibiting general degeneracy/singularity (see condition ( A2) described below), and established global C1,α 

regularity. However, with regard to this type of regularity result for singular or degenerate fully nonlinear equations with Hamiltonian terms under oblique boundary conditions, there are very few results. To the best of our knowledge, the only study in this direction was was carried out by [22], which extended the estimates to the following degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equation with Hamiltonian terms (

|Du |pF (D2u) + h(x)|Du |m = f (x) in Ω,β(x) · Du + γ(x)u = g(x) on ∂Ω,

with p ≥ 0 and 0 < m ≤ 1 + p.Inspired by the work mentioned above, we naturally consider degenerate/singular fully nonlinear elliptic equation with variable coefficient and Hamiltonian terms under oblique boundary condition. Our primary focus is on establishing sharp global C1,α regularity estimate for viscosity solutions to (1.1) in a unified way, provided that Φ( |Du |, ·) satisfies the quite general degeneracy/singularity ( A2) below.
