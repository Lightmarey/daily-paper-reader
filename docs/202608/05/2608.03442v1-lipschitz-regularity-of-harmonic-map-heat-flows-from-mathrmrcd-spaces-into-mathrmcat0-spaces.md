## 摘要
调和映射热流进入完备的CAT(0)空间时，即使目标空间奇异，也存在变分构造，但当源空间也不光滑时，其正则性研究尚不充分。我们在初始数据有界像假设下，证明了从有限维RCD(K,N)空间到完备CAT(0)目标的Korevaar–Schoen能量的EVI梯度流具有正时间局部Lipschitz正则性。所得的表示在空间和时间上联合局部Lipschitz。证明结合了度量Hamilton–Jacobi论证与在RCD源的合适时间切片上应用的椭圆接触估计。

## Abstract
Harmonic map heat flows into complete $\mathrm{CAT}(0)$ spaces admit a variational construction even when the target is singular, but their regularity is less understood when the source is also nonsmooth. We prove positive-time local Lipschitz regularity for the $\mathrm{EVI}$ gradient flow of the Korevaar--Schoen energy from a finite-dimensional $\mathrm{RCD}(K,N)$ space into a complete $\mathrm{CAT}(0)$ target, under a bounded-image assumption on the initial data. The resulting representative is locally Lipschitz jointly in space and time. The proof combines a metric Hamilton--Jacobi argument with an elliptic contact estimate applied on suitable time slices of the $\mathrm{RCD}$ source.
