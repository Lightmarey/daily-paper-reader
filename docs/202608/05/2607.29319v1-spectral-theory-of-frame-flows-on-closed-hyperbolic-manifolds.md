## 摘要
我们证明了双曲流形上标架流的生成元在远离共振垂直线的区域上的预解估计。证明的一个副产品是生成元的最优本质谱间隙性质，从而给出了标架流关于标架丛体积测度指数混合的另一个证明。这将文献[https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08387v2]中三维的结果推广到任意维度。我们广泛使用文献[https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14846]中发展的Borel-Weil演算来克服高维情形下的困难。

## Abstract
We prove a resolvent estimate for the generator of the frame flow on hyperbolic manifolds away from vertical lines of resonances. A byproduct of the proof is an optimal essential spectral gap property for the generator, hence giving another proof of exponential mixing of frame flows with respect to the volume measure of the frame bundle. This extends the result of [https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08387v2] in dimension 3 to any dimension. We make an extensive use of the Borel-Weil calculus developed in [https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14846] to overcome difficulties of this higher-dimensional case.
