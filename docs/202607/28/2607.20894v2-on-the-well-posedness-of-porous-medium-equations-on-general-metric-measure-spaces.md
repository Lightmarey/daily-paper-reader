## 摘要
在一般度量测度空间上，我们发展了与带符号的多孔介质方程及其快速扩散对应物相关的柯西问题的适定性理论：\[ \partial_t u = \mathcal{L}(|u|^{m-1}u), \qquad m>0, \] 其中$\mathcal{L}$是一个对称狄利克雷形式的生成元。我们证明，对于每个初始数据$u_0\in L^{m+1}(M,μ)$，在狄利克雷形式关联的自然能量空间中存在唯一的弱解。证明依赖于罗特方法和单调算子理论，并且仅需要狄利克雷形式及其扩展狄利克雷空间；没有施加额外的正则性或几何假设。因此，该理论适用于广泛的度量测度空间，包括非光滑分形。

## Abstract
On general metric measure spaces, we develop a well-posedness theory for the Cauchy problem associated with the signed porous medium equation and its fast diffusion counterpart \[ \partial_t u = \mathcal{L}(|u|^{m-1}u), \qquad m>0, \] where $\mathcal{L}$ is the generator of a symmetric Dirichlet form. We prove that, for every initial datum $u_0\in L^{m+1}(M,μ)$, there exists a unique weak solution in the natural energy space associated with the Dirichlet form. The proof relies on the Rothe method and the theory of monotone operators, and only requires the Dirichlet form and its extended Dirichlet space; no additional regularity or geometric assumptions are imposed. Consequently, the theory applies to a wide range of metric measure spaces, including non-smooth fractals.
