## 摘要
我们提出了一种用于交错MAC网格上二维外部/内部斯托克斯方程的非拟合边界代数方程方法。通过从自由空间拉普拉斯点阵格林函数构建显式的自由空间速度与压力点阵格林函数对，我们利用仅支持在薄交错边界层上的源来表示均匀场。该公式通过局部插值在切割点处施加物理狄利克雷数据，而采样法向秩更新则消除了与单个或多个障碍物相关的静水零模态。其工作流程与经典边界积分公式类似，外部流动无需人工边界条件，但遵循先离散后表示的路径，且不需要奇异/近奇异求积。得到的稠密边界系统通过GMRES求解，利用基于标量拉普拉斯核的分量式离散Calderón预处理子以及用于快速体积卷积的补零FFT。广泛的数值验证，包括多连通域、窄间隙和莫法特涡旋，证实了离散不可压缩性达到求解器精度，并恢复了预期的莫法特涡旋标度。我们实现了速度和压力的二阶收敛，并将最大离散散度控制在数值精度内。离散Calderón预处理子将条件数降低了几个数量级，并在外部配置中产生了几乎网格无关的条件数，同时对于窄间隙和细网格内部问题仍然有效，尽管要求更高。

## Abstract
We present an unfitted boundary algebraic equation method for the two-dimensional exterior/interior Stokes equations on a staggered MAC grid. By constructing an explicit free-space pair of velocity and pressure lattice Green's functions (LGFs) from free-space Laplace LGFs, we represent homogeneous fields using sources supported exclusively on thin staggered boundary layers. This formulation imposes physical Dirichlet data at cut points via local interpolation, while sampled-normal rank updates remove hydrostatic null modes associated with single or multiple obstacles. The workflow parallels that of classical boundary integral formulations and requires no artificial boundary conditions for exterior flows, but follows a discretize-then-represent route and does not require singular/near-singular quadrature. The resulting dense boundary system is solved via GMRES, utilizing a componentwise discrete Calderón preconditioner built from the scalar Laplace kernel and padded FFTs for fast volume convolutions. Extensive numerical validation, including multiply connected domains, narrow gaps, and Moffatt eddies, confirms discrete incompressibility to solver accuracy and recovers the expected Moffatt eddy scaling. We achieve second-order velocity and pressure convergence and bound maximum discrete divergence within numerical accuracy. The discrete Calderón preconditioner reduces the condition number by orders of magnitude and yields nearly mesh-independent conditioning in exterior configurations, while remaining effective---though more demanding---for narrow-gap and fine-grid interior problems.
