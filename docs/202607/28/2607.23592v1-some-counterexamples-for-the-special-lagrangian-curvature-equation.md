## 摘要
我们为特殊拉格朗日曲率方程（SLCE）构造了三个反例。首先，在二维情形下，我们利用具有恒定正高斯曲率的平行曲面的后焦分支，构造了一个显式的Lipschitz粘性解，该解不是$C^1$的。其次，同样在二维情形下，我们在一个固定矩形上构造了一列光滑容许解，其$C^1$范数一致有界，但在某一点曲率无界；此外，我们证明对于任何$\beta > 1/3$，一致$C^{1,\beta}$估计均不成立。第三，在三维及亚临界相中，我们构造了一个Mooney-Savin型Lipschitz粘性解，其梯度在一个解析曲面上具有跳跃不连续性。这些例子表明了邱和周最近先验估计的尖锐性，揭示了其结构假设——凸性和临界相——是严格必要的。

## Abstract
We construct three counterexamples for the special Lagrangian curvature equation (SLCE). First, in dimension two, we use a post-focal branch of a parallel surface with constant positive Gauss curvature to construct an explicit Lipschitz viscosity solution which is not $C^1$. Second, still in dimension two, we construct a sequence of smooth admissible solutions on a fixed rectangle with uniformly bounded $C^1$-norm but unbounded curvature at one point; furthermore, we show that any uniform $C^{1,β}$ estimate fails for $β> 1/3$. Third, in dimension three and in the subcritical phase, we construct a Mooney-Savin type Lipschitz viscosity solution whose gradient has a jump discontinuity across an analytic surface.   These examples demonstrate the sharpness of the recent a priori estimates by Qiu and Zhou, revealing that their structural assumptions-convexity and the critical phase-are strictly necessary.
