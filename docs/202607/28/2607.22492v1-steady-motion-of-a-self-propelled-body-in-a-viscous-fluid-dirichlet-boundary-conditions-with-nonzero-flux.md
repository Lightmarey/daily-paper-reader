## 摘要
我们研究了一个浸没在不可压缩粘性流体中的刚体在三维外部区域中的稳态自推进运动。在固连参考系中，该问题由定义在固定外部区域上的流-刚体耦合系统描述，其中Navier-Stokes方程与刚体未知的平移和角速度耦合。自推进是通过在物体表面指定边界速度产生的。我们的主要结果断言，在仅假设指定的边界通量足够小的条件下，弱解存在。关键步骤是构造边界数据的无散延拓，该延拓既不依赖于零通量条件，也不依赖于边界数据的小性。因此，我们推广了Galdi的结果[On the steady self-propelled motion of a body in a viscous incompressible fluid. Arch. Rational Mech. Anal., 148 (1999), 53-88中的定理5.1]，该结果中两者都是必需的。

## Abstract
We study the steady self-propelled motion of a rigid body immersed in an incompressible viscous fluid occupying an exterior domain in $\mathbb{R}^3$. In a body-fixed reference frame, the problem is described by a coupled fluid-rigid body system posed in a fixed exterior domain, where the Navier--Stokes equations are coupled with the unknown translational and angular velocities of the rigid body. The self-propulsion is generated by prescribing a boundary velocity on the body surface. Our main result asserts the existence of weak solutions under the sole assumption that the prescribed boundary flux is sufficiently small. The crucial step is the construction of a divergence-free extension of the boundary data that does not rely on either the zero-flux condition or the smallness of the boundary data. As a result, we generalize the result of Galdi [Theorem 5.1 in On the steady self-propelled motion of a body in a viscous incompressible fluid. Arch. Rational Mech. Anal., 148 (1999), 53-88], where both assumptions were required.
