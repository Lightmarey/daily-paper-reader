## 摘要
在本文中，我们研究几乎η-里奇-山本孤子作为耦合到非完美流体的静态球对称黑洞的基本几何调节器。我们证明了标度参数ω(r)由沿径向矢量场的热力学摩擦以及几何耦合到霍金温度所支配：在视界处有α(r_H) S_{tt} = 2πT_H。我们还推导了沿孤子梯度矢量场的泊松方程，并证明了流的运动学膨胀显式依赖于流体的状态方程ρ= γσ。与先验假设几何形状函数的传统方法不同，我们分析证明了几何流通过时间坐标的正则化和满足空间向外展开条件，内生地将黑洞几何转变为可穿越虫洞喉部。当流体在γ= -1时进入暗能量时代并违反零能量条件ρ+ σ< 0时，这种转变发生，孤子严格主导局部曲率梯度ω'(r_H) > f''(r_H)，以保持喉部开放。此外，通过在空间无穷远处平滑衰减，孤子保留了精确的宇宙时空。最后，通过张量扰动分析，我们证明了几何流引入了一种局域耗散机制，扰动演化简化为阻尼波动方程，在流形上施加了几何阻力。

## Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the almost $η$-Ricci-Yamabe soliton as a fundamental geometric regulator for a static, spherically symmetric black hole coupled to an imperfect fluid. We have shown that the scaling parameter $ω(r)$ is governed by thermodynamic friction along the radial vector field, and the geometric coupling with the Hawking temperature: $α(r_H) S_{tt} = 2πT_H$ at the horizon. We also derive the Poisson equation along the gradient vector field of the soliton and prove that the flow's kinematic expansion is explicitly dependent on the fluid's equation of state $ρ= γσ$. Diverging from traditional methodologies that assume a geometric shape function apriori, we analytically proved the geometric flow endogenously transitions the black hole geometry into a traversable wormhole throat by regularizing of temporal coordinate and satisfying spatial flare-out condition. This transition occurs when fluid enters the dark energy era at $γ= -1$ and violates the Null Energy Condition $ρ+ σ< 0$, with the soliton strictly dominating the local curvature gradient $ω^{\prime}(r_H) > f^{\prime\prime}(r_H)$, to keep the throat open. Moreover, by smoothly attenuating at spatial infinity, the soliton preserves the exact cosmological spacetime. Finally, through tensorial perturbation analysis, we demonstrate that the geometric flow introduces a localized dissipative mechanism, that the perturbation evolution reduces to damped wave equation, imposing geometric drag on the manifold.
