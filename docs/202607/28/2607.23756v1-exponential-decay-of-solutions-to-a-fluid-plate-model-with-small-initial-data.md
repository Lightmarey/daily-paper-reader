## 摘要
我们考虑一个三维流固耦合问题，该问题将随时间变化区域内的不可压缩Navier-Stokes方程与施加在流体移动上边界上的平方根阻尼板方程耦合在一起。我们先验地证明了，对于在适当的Sobolev空间中足够小的初值，强解是指数衰减的。证明结合了高阶能量估计、Stokes型正则性界以及一个在小假设下闭合的非线性自举方案。

## Abstract
We consider a three-dimensional fluid-structure interaction problem coupling the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations in a time-dependent domain with a square-root damped plate equation, which is posed on the moving upper boundary of the fluid. We prove, a priori, the exponential decay of strong solutions for initial data that are sufficiently small in a suitable Sobolev space. The proof combines higher-order energy estimates, Stokes-type regularity bounds, and a nonlinear bootstrap scheme that closes under the smallness assumption.
