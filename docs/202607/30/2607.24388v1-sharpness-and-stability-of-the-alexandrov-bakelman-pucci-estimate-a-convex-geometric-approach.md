## 摘要
基于G. Talenti在1981年的工作，该工作为Monge-Ampère方程的光滑解建立了比较原理以及相关的二维先验估计，本文探讨了这些与Blaschke-Santaló不等式及其K. Mahler (1939)的反向形式之间的联系。更具体地说，本文证明了Talenti先验估计之一的定量版本。我们利用凸几何方法探索Talenti分析框架与Mahler/Blaschke-Santaló不等式之间的相互作用，展示了如何利用这种联系来获得对椭圆偏微分方程的Alexandrov-Bakelman-Pucci (ABP)极大值原理的尖锐洞察。

## Abstract
Based on a 1981 work by G. Talenti, which established a comparison principle for smooth solutions of the Monge-Ampère equation alongside related two-dimensional a priori estimates, this paper explores the connection to the Blaschke-Santaló inequality and its reverse form by K. Mahler (1939). More specifically, in this work, a quantitative version of one of Talenti's a priori estimates is proved. We exploit the interplay between Talenti's analytical framework and Mahler/Blaschke-Santaló inequalities via convex geometry, demonstrating how this link can be utilized to gain sharp insights into the Alexandrov-Bakelman-Pucci (ABP) maximum principle for elliptic PDEs.
