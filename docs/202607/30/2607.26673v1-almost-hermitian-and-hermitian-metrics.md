## 摘要
我们描述了埃尔米特几何中与陈联络的标量曲率相关的三个问题，旨在识别和构造规范度量。选择它们作为不同技术自然交互的玩具例子：偏微分方程的解析方法、动量映射概念和变分方法。

## Abstract
We describe three problems in Hermitian geometry related to the scalar curvature of the Chern connection, aimed at identifying and constructing canonical metrics. They are chosen as toy examples where different techniques naturally interact: analytic methods for partial differential equations, the notion of momentum map, and variational methods.
