## 摘要
本文分析了在二维多边形区域中，由Stokes流体和线弹性固体组成的流固耦合（FSI）问题的解的正则性，其中界面与区域边界相交。主要挑战来自区域角点处的几何奇异性导致的解的正则性受限。为此，我们引入了定制的加权Sobolev空间，记作 $\mathbf{H}^{k,l}_{\boldsymbolβ}$，并在此空间上建立了一个新的解算子框架。该框架为正则性分析中将耦合的FSI系统解耦为可处理的流体和弹性固体子问题提供了关键思路。作为主要结果，我们证明了在加权Sobolev空间 $\mathbf{H}^{2,2}_{\boldsymbolβ}$ 中解的存在唯一性。

## Abstract
This paper analyzes the regularity of solutions to a fluid-structure interaction (FSI) problem involving a Stokesian fluid and a linear elastic solid in a two-dimensional polygonal domain, seperated by an interface which intersects the boundary of the domain. The main challenges arise from the limited solution regularity caused by geometric singularities at the domain corners. To address this, we introduce tailored weighted Sobolev spaces, denoted by $\mathbf{H}^{k,l}_{\boldsymbolβ}$, and a novel solution operator framework on these spaces. This framework provides the key insight for decoupling the coupled FSI system into tractable fluid and elastic solid subproblems in the regularity analysis. As our main result, we prove the existence and uniqueness of a solution in the weighted Sobolev space $\mathbf{H}^{2,2}_{\boldsymbolβ}$.
