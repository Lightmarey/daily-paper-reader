## 摘要
我们研究了在具有Navier滑移边界条件的光滑、有界、连通区域上的二维Navier-Stokes方程的连续数据同化，不使用内部观测。可用数据仅包括在非空相对开子集 $\Gamma\subset\partial\Omega$ 上的切向速度的有限维测量。我们证明，由足够精细的观测构建的足够强的边界反馈，为同化误差产生一个强制谱间隙。该极限间隙与通过在 $\Gamma$ 上施加齐次Dirichlet条件得到的混合边界问题的间隙相同，且被证明是 $\nu$ 阶的。将这种反馈引起的强制性与关于参考解长时间平均对称梯度能量的非线性误差产生估计相结合，我们得到了指数同步的充分判据。我们在无强迫情况下验证了该判据，当未受扰动的Navier滑移形式具有 $\mathrm{L}^2$ 谱间隙时对于足够小的强迫，在没有切向刚体运动的区域上对于足够大的粘度，以及在存在正边界摩擦时对于足够大的粘度。我们还处理了允许切向刚体运动的区域上的完美滑移情况，其中在强迫的非刚体无散分量满足小量条件下同步成立。

## Abstract
We study continuous data assimilation for the two-dimensional Navier--Stokes equations on a smooth, bounded, connected domain with Navier-slip boundary conditions, using no interior observations. The available data consist only of finite-dimensional measurements of the tangential velocity on a non-empty relatively open subset $Γ\subset\partialΩ$. We prove that sufficiently strong boundary feedback, constructed from sufficiently fine observations, generates a coercive spectral gap for the assimilation error. The limiting gap is identified with that of a mixed-boundary problem obtained by imposing a homogeneous Dirichlet condition on $Γ$, and is shown to be of order $ν$. Combining this feedback-induced coercivity with an estimate of the non-linear error production in terms of the long-time averaged symmetric-gradient energy of the reference solution, we obtain a sufficient criterion for exponential synchronisation. We verify this criterion in the unforced case, for sufficiently small forcing when the unnudged Navier-slip form has an $\mathrm{L}^2$ spectral gap, for sufficiently large viscosity on domains without tangential rigid motions, and for sufficiently large viscosity in the presence of positive boundary friction. We also treat perfect slip on domains admitting tangential rigid motions, where synchronisation follows under a smallness condition on the non-rigid solenoidal component of the forcing.
