## 摘要
我们讨论了不可压缩纳维-斯托克斯方程（速度方程）与其在全空间中的涡量形式之间的一个有趣区别。我们证明，如果初始涡量具有高斯界，则该界在强解的最大生命跨度内得以保持。然而，速度方程并不具备相同的性质。事实上，由“良好局部化”初始值产生的速度方程$L^p$强解在无穷远处通常表现得像拉普拉斯基本解的（阶数$\geq3$）导数。为证明这一点，我们推导出了一个直到最大生命跨度的清晰展开：
\begin{align}
u(x,t)=-\nabla\sum_{|\alpha|=0}^{d-1}\frac{(-1)^{|\alpha|}}{\alpha!}\partial^\alpha\partial_{i,j}^2\Gamma(x)\int_0^t{\rm M}_\alpha^{i,j}(s){\rm d}s+O\big(|x|^{-2d-1}\big)\nonumber
\end{align}
其中 ${\rm M}_\alpha^{i,j}(t):=\int_{\mathbb R^d}y^\alpha u^i(y,t)u^j(y,t){\rm d}y$，$\Gamma$ 是拉普拉斯基本解。这改进了 L. Brandolese 和 F. Vigneron \cite{BV07} 给出的一阶展开。

## Abstract
We discuss an interesting distinction between the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations (the velocity equations) and its vorticity form in whole space. We show that if the initial vorticity has a Gaussian bound then the bound is inherited up to the maximal lifespan of the strong solution. However, it turns out that the velocity equations don not share the same property. In fact, $L^p$-strong solutions to the velocity equations arising from ``well-localized" initial value generally behave at infinity like derivatives (of order $\geq3$) of the fundamental solution of Laplacian. To show this, a clean expansion up to maximal lifespan is derived :   \begin{align}   u(x,t)=-\nabla\sum_{|α|=0}^{d-1}\frac{(-1)^{|α|}}{α!}\partial^α\partial_{i,j}^2Γ(x)\int_0^t{\rm M}_α^{i,j}(s){\rm d}s+O\big(|x|^{-2d-1}\big)\nonumber   \end{align} where ${\rm M}_α^{i,j}(t):=\int_{\mathbb R^d}y^αu^i(y,t)u^j(y,t){\rm d}y$ and $Γ$ is the fundamental solution of Laplacian. This improves the first order expansion given by L. Brandolese and F. Vigneron \cite{BV07}.
