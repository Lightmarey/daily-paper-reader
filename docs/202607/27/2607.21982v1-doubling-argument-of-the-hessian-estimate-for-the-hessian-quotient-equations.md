## 摘要
在本文中，我们建立了一个加倍论证，以获得Hessian商方程 $\frac{σ_n}{σ_k}(D^2u) = f(x,u,Du)$ 在 $k=n-1$ 和 $k=n-2$ 情形下凸解的Hessian估计，条件是在 $Du$ 变量上 $1/f$ 是凹的。特别地，我们的方法是逐点的，并且不利用勒让德变换或基于积分的局部极大值原理。我们提供了一个反例，表明如果不对 $f$ 在 $Du$ 变量上施加结构假设，内部估计可能失效。最后，我们将加倍论证推广到一般的Hessian商方程 $\frac{σ_l}{σ_k}(D^2u) = f(x,u,Du)$，其中 $k \in \{l-1, l-2\}$，在 $f$ 关于 $Du$ 变量满足类似结构条件的基础上，再加上由Lu-Tsai 2026引入的算子的附加结构凹性假设。

## Abstract
In this paper, we establish a doubling argument to obtain Hessian estimates for convex solutions to the Hessian quotient equation $\frac{σ_n}{σ_k}(D^2u) = f(x,u,Du)$ for $k=n-1$ and $k=n-2$ under the condition that $1/f$ is concave in the $Du$ variable. In particular, our approach is pointwise and does not make use of the Legendre transform or integral-based local maximum principles. We provide a counterexample demonstrating that interior estimates can fail if no structural assumption is imposed on $f$ in the $Du$ variable. Finally, we extend our doubling argument to general Hessian quotient equations $\frac{σ_l}{σ_k}(D^2u) = f(x,u,Du)$ for $k \in \{l-1, l-2\}$, under a similar structural condition imposed on $f$ in the $Du$ variable, alongside an additional structural concavity assumption on the operator introduced by Lu-Tsai 2026.
