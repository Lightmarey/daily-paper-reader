## 摘要
我们证明了在任意分层李群上，对于水平q-拉普拉斯算子（也称为这些群上的q-子拉普拉斯算子）的一类具有指数非线性的拟线性方程正弱解的存在性。该非线性项结合了一个凹项和一个具有指数增长的部分，该增长可以相对于分层李群上洛伦兹空间的Trudinger-Moser不等式是次临界、临界或超临界的。我们在本文中证明了该不等式的一个版本。由于该问题在自然能量空间中不是变分的，因此经典的极小化或临界点技术不能直接应用。随后，通过精心设计的应用于该设定的Galerkin方法，得到了所得半线性方程的正解。我们的主定理恢复了在完整范围q∈(1,∞)内关于ℝ^n的先前已知结果，以及在海森堡群ℍ^n上关于q=2n+2的先前已知结果，并且在此将其推广到完整范围q∈(1,∞)。还讨论了其他特殊且基础的情形，包括Engel群。

## Abstract
We prove the existence of positive weak solutions for a quasilinear equation with exponential nonlinearity on arbitrary stratified Lie groups for \emph{horizontal $q$-Laplacians,} also called $q$-sub-Laplacians on these groups. The nonlinearity combines a concave term with an exponential growth that can be subcritical, critical, or supercritical with respect to the Trudinger--Moser inequality for Lorentz spaces on stratified Lie groups. We prove a version of this inequality in this paper. Since the problem is not variational in the natural energy space, classical minimisation or critical-point techniques do not apply directly. Positive solutions to the resulting semilinear equation are then obtained via a carefully designed Galerkin method applied to this setting. Our main theorem recovers previous known results on $\mathbb{R}^n$ in the complete range $q\in (1,\infty),$ and the previous known result on the Heisenberg group $\mathbb{H}^n$ for $q=2n+2,$ and is extended here in the full range $q\in (1,\infty)$. Other particular and fundamental cases, including the Engel group, are discussed.
