## 摘要
我们研究了全局双曲洛伦兹流形上半线性波动方程中光滑非线性的恢复问题。我们的方法将高阶线性化与畸变平面波和高斯束相结合，利用三波、四波和多波相互作用来恢复不同的泰勒系数。

## Abstract
We study the recovery of a smooth nonlinearity in semilinear wave equations on globally hyperbolic Lorentzian manifolds. Our approach combines higher-order linearization with distorted plane waves and Gaussian beams, using three-wave, four-wave, and m-wave interactions to recover different Taylor coefficients.
