## 摘要
本文研究有界区域中各向异性极小曲面方程的Dirichlet问题。在非负边界各向异性平均曲率的自然假设下，我们建立了连续边界数据下Dirichlet问题的唯一可解性。为实现这一点，我们建立了一个必要的先验梯度估计，该估计还使我们能够证明具有线性增长的整体解的Bernstein定理的弱形式。此外，利用变分学中的直接方法，我们证明了在$BV(Ω)$中具有$L^1(∂Ω)$边界数据的广义极小值的存在性和局部Lipschitz正则性。我们还发现，这一变分公式自然地导出了一个Neumann边界条件，从几何上解释了为何Neumann问题不需要边界曲率约束。

## Abstract
This paper investigates the Dirichlet problem for the anisotropic minimal surface equation in a bounded domain. Under the natural assumption of non-negative boundary anisotropic mean curvature, we establish the unique solvability of the Dirichlet problem for continuous boundary data. To achieve this, an essential a priori gradient estimate is established, which also allows us to prove a weak version of Bernstein's theorem for entire solutions with linear growth. Moreover, using the direct method in the calculus of variations, we prove the existence and local Lipschitz regularity of generalized minimizers in $BV(Ω)$ with $L^1(\partialΩ)$ boundary data. We also find that this variational formulation naturally yields a Neumann boundary condition, geometrically explaining why the Neumann problem requires no boundary curvature constraints.
