## 摘要
设 n≥3 且 1<p<n。我们证明了上半空间上临界Sobolev迹不等式的一个定量稳定性估计。更精确地说，Sobolev迹亏缺控制着到迹气泡流形的梯度距离的 max{2,p} 次幂。证明的一个核心部分是迹气泡的谱非退化性：线性化加权Steklov问题的前两个特征空间恰好是振幅、伸缩和切向平移模式。

## Abstract
Let \(n\ge3\) and \(1<p<n\). We prove a quantitative stability estimate for the critical Sobolev trace inequality on the upper half-space. More precisely, the Sobolev trace deficit controls the \(\max\{2,p\}\)-th power of the gradient distance to the manifold of trace bubbles. A central part of the proof is the spectral nondegeneracy of the trace bubbles: the first two eigenspaces of the linearized weighted Steklov problem are exactly the amplitude, dilation, and tangential translation modes.
