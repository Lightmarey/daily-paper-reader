## 摘要
证明了当$k>n/2$时，在（非光滑且非均匀）$k$-凸（也称为$k$-超凸）区域上，对于任意有限Borel测度作为右端项，边界数据取自该区域闭包上的一个$k$-凸连续函数，该$k$-Hessian测度的Dirichlet问题存在唯一解。对于严格$k$-凸区域（即存在强障碍函数时），仅需要连续的边界数据即可。

## Abstract
It is proved for $k>n/2$ that the Dirichlet problem of the $k$-Hessian measure on a (nonsmooth and nonuniformly) $k$-convex (also known as $k$-hyperconvex) domain for any finite Borel measure as right-hand side and boundary data taken from a $k$-convex and continuous function on the closure of the domain has a unique solution. Merely continuous boundary data are sufficient for strictly $k$-convex domains (i.e., if there exist strong barriers).
