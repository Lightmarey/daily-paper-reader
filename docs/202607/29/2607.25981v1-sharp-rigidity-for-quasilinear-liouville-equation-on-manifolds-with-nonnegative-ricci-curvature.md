## 摘要
我们研究了在具有非负Ricci曲率的完备非紧Riemann流形上的拟线性Liouville方程\[ -\Delta_n u=e^u \]。我们的第一个结果表明，如果解$u$满足最优对数下界\[ u(x)\ge -\frac{n^2}{n-1}\log r(x)+o(\log r(x)) \quad \text{当 }r(x)\to+\infty,\]那么该流形等距于欧氏空间，且$u$是一个标准泡沫解。假设中的主项系数和余项都是尖锐的。证明的关键在于对数下界与解的总体积的尖锐上界之间的联系。我们还提出了一个关于解的对数下衰减与底层几何之间相互作用的猜想，并在$n=2$以及$n\ge 3$且在加强假设下证明了该猜想。

## Abstract
We study the quasilinear Liouville equation \[ -Δ_n u=e^u \] on complete noncompact Riemannian manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature. Our first result shows that, if a solution $u$ satisfies the optimal logarithmic lower bound \[ u(x)\ge -\frac{n^2}{n-1}\log r(x)+o(\log r(x)) \quad \text{as }r(x)\to+\infty, \] then the underlying manifold is isometric to the Euclidean space and $u$ is a standard bubble solution. Both the leading coefficient and the remainder term in the assumption are sharp. The key ingredient in the proof is the connection between the logarithmic lower bound and a sharp upper bound on the total volume of the solution.   We also formulate a conjecture concerning the interaction between the sub-logarithmic decay of solutions and the underlying geometry, and prove it for $n=2$, as well as for $n\ge 3$ under a strengthened assumption.
