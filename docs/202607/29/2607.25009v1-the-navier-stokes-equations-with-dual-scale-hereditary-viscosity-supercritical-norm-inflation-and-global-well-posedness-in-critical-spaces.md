## 摘要
本文研究了由双尺度遗传记忆控制不可压缩流体流动的柯西问题，该问题代表了经典Navier-Stokes方程的一种粘弹性变体，捕捉了异常动量传输。非局部耗散破坏了精确的全局尺度不变性，要求在Hörmander类$S^{-2}_{1,0}$中进行伪微分分析，其中空间梯度引入了分数阶时间惩罚。我们严格建立了$L^q-L^p$衰减估计，并确定了临界Lebesgue阈值$p_c = N (\frac{1+α_\infty}{1-α_\infty})$。在超临界区域$1 < p < p_c$中，我们通过将频率调制的双线性流直接映射到傅里叶空间来绕过空间定位的损失；利用Bernstein不等式，我们证明了在原点处的瞬时范数膨胀，并确认了内在的不适定性。相反，在拓扑极限$p \to \infty$中，我们证明了双尺度记忆在结构上阻止了传统流体在最大临界Besov空间$\dot{B}^{-1}_{\infty, \infty}$中通常观察到的塌缩。通过利用Bony的仿微分演算中的非对称插值，我们证明了时间平滑性压倒了高-高对流共振级联。这种精细的分析平衡将不适定性限制在Besov拓扑的不可分离高频尾部，从而为在$\dot{B}^{-κ}_{\infty, \infty}(\mathbb{R}^N)$中具有高频依附的小初值建立了全局时间Hadamard适定性，该适定性区域严格宽于可分离的little Besov闭包$\dot{b}^{-κ}_{\infty, \infty}(\mathbb{R}^N)$，其中$κ= \frac{1-α_\infty}{1+α_\infty}$。

## Abstract
This manuscript investigates the Cauchy problem for an incompressible fluid flow governed by a dual-scale hereditary memory, representing a viscoelastic variant of the classical Navier-Stokes equations that captures anomalous momentum transport. The non-local dissipation breaks exact global scale invariance, dictating a pseudo-differential analysis within the Hörmander class $S^{-2}_{1,0}$ where the spatial gradient induces a fractional temporal penalty. We rigorously establish $L^q-L^p$ decay estimates and identify the critical Lebesgue threshold $p_c = N (\frac{1+α_\infty}{1-α_\infty})$. In the supercritical regime $1 < p < p_c$, we bypass the loss of spatial localization by mapping the frequency-modulated bilinear flow directly into Fourier space; by utilizing Bernstein's inequalities, we prove instantaneous norm inflation at the origin and confirm intrinsic ill-posedness. Conversely, in the topological limit $p \to \infty$, we demonstrate that the dual-scale memory structurally prevents the collapse traditionally observed for classical fluids within the maximal critical Besov space $\dot{B}^{-1}_{\infty, \infty}$. By exploiting an asymmetric interpolation within Bony's para-differential calculus, we prove that the temporal smoothing overpowers the high-high convective resonant cascade. This delicate analytical balance confines ill-posedness to the non-separable high-frequency tail of the Besov topology, thereby establishing global-in-time Hadamard well-posedness for small initial data possessing high-frequency adherence within $\dot{B}^{-κ}_{\infty, \infty}(\mathbb{R}^N)$, a well-posedness regime strictly broader than the separable little Besov closure $\dot{b}^{-κ}_{\infty, \infty}(\mathbb{R}^N)$, where $κ= \frac{1-α_\infty}{1+α_\infty}$.
