## 摘要
设$K\subset \RN$为内部包含原点的凸体，$\hK{\cdot}$为其闵可夫斯基泛函。本文发展了一个基于恒等式的框架，用于建立与各向异性径向导数$$ \mathcal R_K(u)(x)=\frac{x\cdot\nabla u(x)}{\hK{x}}, \quad x\in\RN\setminus\{o\} $$相关的尖锐各向异性$L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg不等式。本工作的一个关键点在于不假设$K$关于原点对称。因此，闵可夫斯基泛函$\hK{\cdot}$不必是偶函数，通常基于范数的各向异性方法不能直接适用。主要工具是带有显式非负余项的各类各向异性$L^2$-Hardy与$L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg恒等式。这些恒等式导出了尖锐的各向异性$L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg不等式，其最佳常数依赖于参数$(a,b)\in\mathbb R^2$所在的区域。我们还在自然完备化空间中研究了尖锐常数的可达性，并得到了相应的极值函数。作为进一步推论，我们导出了尖锐的各向异性海森伯格型不确定性原理以及极大型各向异性梯度不等式。当$K$为欧几里得单位球时，我们的结果恢复了经典的欧几里得$L^2$理论；当$K$关于原点对称时，它们与通常基于范数的各向异性框架一致。特别地，本文结果将尖锐的$L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg理论推广到了内部包含原点的一般凸体，此时闵可夫斯基泛函可能非偶。

## Abstract
Let $K\subset \RN$ be a convex body containing the origin in its interior, and let $\hK{\cdot}$ be its Minkowski functional. In this paper, we develop an identity-based framework for sharp anisotropic $L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg inequalities associated with the anisotropic radial derivative $$ \mathcal R_K(u)(x)=\frac{x\cdot\nabla u(x)}{\hK{x}}, \quad x\in\RN\setminus\{o\}. $$ A key point of the present work is that $K$ is not assumed to be origin-symmetric. Consequently, the Minkowski functional $\hK{\cdot}$ need not be even, and the usual norm-based anisotropic arguments do not apply directly. The main tools are anisotropic $L^2$-Hardy and $L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg identities with explicit nonnegative remainders. These identities yield sharp anisotropic $L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg inequalities whose best constants depend on the parameter region of $(a,b)\in\mathbb R^2$. We also study the attainability of the sharp constants in a natural completion space and obtain the corresponding extremal functions. As further consequences, we derive sharp anisotropic Heisenberg-type uncertainty principles and max-type anisotropic gradient inequalities. When $K$ is the Euclidean unit ball, our results recover the classical Euclidean $L^2$ theory; when $K$ is origin-symmetric, they are consistent with the usual norm-based anisotropic framework. In particular, the present results extend the sharp $L^2$-Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg theory to general convex bodies containing the origin in their interiors, for which the Minkowski functional may be non-even.
