## 摘要
本文研究${\mathbb R}^3$中光滑闭超曲面领结邻域内的原始方程。利用最大$L_q$-正则性和流体静力学亥姆霍兹投影的框架，我们建立了强解的存在性、唯一性和正则性结果。在这些局部适定性结果的基础上，我们推导出合适的先验估计，并在不对初始数据施加任何小性假设的情况下证明了强解的全局存在性。此外，我们还证明了这些解在时间和空间上是联合$C^\infty$的。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study the primitive equations in a collar neighborhood of a smooth closed hypersurface in ${\mathbb R}^3$. Using the framework of maximal $L_q$-regularity and the hydrostatic Helmholtz projection, we establish existence, uniqueness, and regularity results for strong solutions. Building on these local well-posedness results, we derive suitable a priori estimates and prove the global existence of strong solutions without imposing any smallness assumptions for the initial data. Moreover, we show that the solutions are $C^\infty$ jointly in time and space.
