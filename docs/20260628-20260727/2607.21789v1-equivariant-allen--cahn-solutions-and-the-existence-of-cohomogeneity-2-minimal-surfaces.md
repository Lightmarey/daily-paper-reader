## 摘要
我们为闭黎曼流形上具有等距作用的李群的等变Allen-Cahn解建立了正则性理论。当作用的余齐性介于3和7之间时，我们证明了一列具有一致有界能量和等变指标的等变Allen-Cahn解收敛到具有最优正则性的嵌入极小超曲面，这意味着奇点集至少是余维数7且位于所有非主轨道的并集中。当余齐性为2且作用无例外轨道时，我们证明了同样的结果，但极小超曲面可能是浸入的。因此，任何具有余齐性2李群作用且无例外轨道的闭黎曼流形都承认一个具有最优正则性的极小超曲面。关键工具是Chodosh-Mantoulidis的正则性理论，该理论建立在Wang-Wei工作的基础上。然而，我们将他们的论证适应于具有漂移拉普拉斯算子的修正Allen-Cahn方程。我们还证明了当极限极小超曲面光滑时，适当的指标界成立。我们还通过定义等变山路不变量和等变Allen-Cahn p-宽度，扩展了Guaraco和Gaspar-Guaraco的Allen-Cahn方程的变分构造。这建立在Gromov的工作基础上，并且是Wang在Almgren-Pitts设置中的等遗体积极谱的Allen-Cahn类似物。我们证明当ε趋于0时，等变Allen-Cahn p-宽度收敛到由Wang定义的等变p-宽度。

## Abstract
We develop a regularity theory for equivariant Allen--Cahn solutions on closed Riemannian manifolds with a Lie group acting isometrically. When the cohomogeneity of the action is between $3$ and $7$, we show that a sequence of equivariant Allen--Cahn solutions with uniformly bounded energy and equivariant index converge to embedded minimal hypersurfaces with optimal regularity, meaning that the singular set is at least codimension $7$ and lies in the union of all non-principal orbits. When the cohomogeneity is $2$ and the action has no exceptional orbits, we show the same result but the minimal hypersurfaces may be immersed. As a result, any closed Riemmanian manifold with cohomogeneity $2$ Lie group action and no exceptional orbits admits a minimal hypersurface with optimal regularity. A key tool is the regularity theory of Chodosh--Mantoulidis, building on the work of Wang--Wei. However, we adapt their arguments to a modified Allen--Cahn equation with a drift Laplacian. We also show that appropriate index bounds hold for the limiting minimal hypersurface when it is smooth.   We also extend the variational constructions of solutions of the Allen--Cahn equation of Guaraco and Gaspar--Guaraco by defining an equivariant mountain pass invariant, as well as the equivariant Allen--Cahn $p$-widths. This builds on the work of Gromov and is the Allen--Cahn parallel to Wang's equivariant volume spectrum in the Almgren-Pitts setting. We show that in the limit as $ε$ tends to $0$, the equivariant Allen--Cahn $p$-widths converge to the equivariant $p$-widths, as defined by Wang.
