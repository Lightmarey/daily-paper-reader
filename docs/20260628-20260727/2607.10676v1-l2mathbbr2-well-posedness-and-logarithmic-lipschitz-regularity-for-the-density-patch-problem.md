## 摘要
我们研究了二维非齐次不可压缩Navier-Stokes系统真空下的密度斑块问题，初始数据由Lipschitz密度斑块和无散度速度场组成，速度场属于$L^2(\mathbb{R}^2)$。我们在自然能量水平上建立了解的唯一性，从而得出$L^2$数据的全局适定性。此外，我们证明了速度场的对数Lipschitz估计，将Chemin-Lerner的经典结果推广到非齐次情形。因此，对于任意$\varepsilon \in (0,1)$，相关的流属于$L^\infty_t C_x^{1-\varepsilon}$，确保斑块边界保持Hausdorff维数为$1$的连续曲线，从而在所有时刻保持其初始维数。

## Abstract
We study the density patch problem for the two-dimensional inhomogeneous incompressible Navier--Stokes system with vacuum, for initial data consisting of a Lipschitz density patch and a divergence-free velocity field in $L^2(\mathbb{R}^2)$.   We establish uniqueness of solutions at the natural energy level, thereby concluding the global well-posedness for $L^2$ data.   Furthermore, we prove a log-Lipschitz estimate for the velocity field, extending the classical result of Chemin-Lerner to the inhomogeneous setting. As a consequence, the associated flow belongs to $L^\infty_t C_x^{1-\varepsilon}$. for any $\varepsilon \in (0,1)$, ensuring that the patch boundary remains a continuous curve of Hausdorff dimension $1$, thus preserving its initial dimension for all time.
