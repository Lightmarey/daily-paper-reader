## 摘要
我们考虑三维不可压缩Navier-Stokes方程温和解$v$的条件正则性，其中初始数据$v_0\in \dot{H}^{\frac 1 2}$且涡量$\Omega_0\in L^{r_0}$，$r_0\in (1,2)$。我们证明：如果与初始数据$v_0$相关的解在有限时间$T^\ast$爆破，则对任意$2<p<\infty$和任意单位向量$e\in\mathbb{R}^3$，积分$$\int_0^{T^\ast}\left\Vert (v(t)|e)_{\mathbb{R}^3}\right\Vert_{\dot{B}^{\frac 1 2+\frac 2 p}_{2,\infty}}^p{\rm d}t$$在$T^\ast$处爆破。该结论改进了Chemin等人(Arch Ration Mech Anal 224(3):871-905, 2017)和Han等人(Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 231:939-970, 2019)的最新结果。

## Abstract
We consider the conditional regularity of the mild solution $v$ of the $3-D$ incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with initial data $v_0\in \dot{H}^{\frac 1 2}$ and vorticity $Ω_0\in L^{r_0}$ for some $r_0\in (1,2)$. We prove that if the solution associated with initial data $v_0$ blows up at a finite time $T^\ast$, then for any $2<p<\infty$, and any unit vectors $e$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$, the integral   $$\int_0^{T^\ast}\left\Vert (v(t)|e)_{\mathbb{R}^3}\right\Vert_{\dot{B}^{\frac 1 2+\frac 2 p}_{2,\infty}}^p{\rm d}t$$ blows up at $T^\ast$. The conclusion improves the recent results in Chemin et al. (Arch Ration Mech Anal 224(3):871-905, 2017) and Han et al. (Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 231:939-970, 2019).
