## 摘要
建立具有大初始条件的三维Navier-Stokes方程解的整体光滑性仍是一个长期未决的开放问题。在本文中，我们证明，由适当选择的迁移噪声——一种物理动机的随机扰动——驱动的三维Navier-Stokes方程以高概率允许整体光滑解。这种正则化效应对于任意大球体内具有正光滑性的次临界函数空间中的初始数据一致成立。

## Abstract
Establishing the global-in-time smoothness of solutions to the 3D Navier-Stokes equations (NSEs) with large initial data remains a long-standing open problem. In this paper, we prove that the 3D NSEs driven by a suitably chosen transport noise -- a physically motivated stochastic perturbation -- admit global-in-time smooth solutions with high probability. This regularizing effect holds uniformly for initial data in arbitrarily large balls of subcritical function spaces with positive smoothness.
