## 摘要
我们证明了作为涉及曲率导数的高阶泛函的 $L^2$-梯度流而获得的一大类曲线和网络的高阶几何流的短时存在性和在参数化意义下的唯一性。此外，我们通过能量方法证明了一个长时间存在性结果。

## Abstract
We show short-time existence and uniqueness up to reparametrization for a large class of higher-order geometric flows of curves and networks obtained as $L^2$-gradient flow of higher-order functionals involving derivatives of the curvature. Additionally, we prove a long-time existence result via energy methods.
