## 摘要
在这篇笔记中，我们提供了一个关于Leray著名的结构定理的新结果，该定理涉及纳维-斯托克斯方程的弱解。我们能够给出正则性无界区间左端点的一个新估计。

## Abstract
In this note we furnish a new result concerning the well known théorème de structure by Leray related to a weak solution to the Navier-Stokes equations. We are able to furnish a new estimate on the left endpoint of the unbounded interval of regularity.
