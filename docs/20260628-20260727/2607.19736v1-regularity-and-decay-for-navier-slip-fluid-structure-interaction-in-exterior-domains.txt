REGULARITY AND DECAY FOR NAVIER-SLIP FLUID-STRUCTURE INTERACTION IN EXTERIOR DOMAINS 

BUM JA JIN ABSTRACT . We study the linearized motion of a spherical rigid body immersed in an incompressible viscous fluid occupying the whole space, under Navier slip boundary conditions with friction. While semigroup regularity and decay esti-mates are well understood for the no-slip case, such results have been limited to bounded domains in the slip setting. By exploiting the geometric regularity of the sphere, we establish strong Lq -regularity, bounded analyticity, and sharp 

Lq − Lr estimates for the corresponding fluid-structure operator. These results would provide a foundation for constructing strong W 2,1 

> q

solutions and Kato-type solutions to the corresponding nonlinear system. 

1. Introduction 

We consider a homogeneous rigid body immersed in a viscous, incompressible fluid occupying the entire space R3. Let v and π denote the velocity and pressure of the fluid, respectively. Let B ⊂ R3 represent the initial region occupied by the rigid body, and define the fluid domain as Ω := R3 \ B. At any time t > 0,the body occupies the region B(t), and its center of mass is denoted by h(t). The translational velocity is ℓ(t) = ˙h(t) and the angular velocity is ω(t). Consequently, the time-dependent fluid domain is given by 

Ω( t) = R3 \ B(t).

For the sake of simplicity, we assume that the densities of both the fluid and the rigid body are equal to 1 throughout this paper (i.e., ρF = 1 and ρB = 1 ). In particular, we consider the case where the rigid body is a ball centered at the origin. However, we note that the general case where the densities may differ is discussed in Remark 3.2. Previous studies have investigated this operator under no-slip boundary condi-tions (see [17, 32, 42, 46, 26]), and more recently under Navier-slip conditions in bounded domains [3]. Before stating our main results, we briefly recall some related works on fluid structure interaction under Navier-slip boundary conditions. Let O be a bounded domain in R3 with B ⊂ O. The resolvent problem for the fluid–structure operator under Navier-slip boundary conditions on the bounded fluid region O \ B has been studied in [3]. The corresponding fluid-structure op-erator generates an analytic semigroup with exponential stability (see [3, Theorem 4.7]), and the system enjoys the maximal regularity property (see [3, Theorem 4.9]). Moreover, there exists a unique strong solution u ∈ Lqt (W2,p x ) ∩ W1,q t (Lpx)

to the nonlinear problem (see [3, Theorem 2.1]). In this paper, we study the linear system (1.8) in the exterior domain Ω := 

R3 \ B, where the body B is contained in a ball BR0 for some R0 > 0. The sys-tem is supplemented with the conditions (C1)–(C3), representing the initial state, boundary behavior, and the condition at infinity, respectively. We establish strong 

Lq regularity and prove that the associated fluid-structure semigroup is bounded and analytic. In addition, we derive Lr-Lq decay estimates. The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we introduce the general notation and state the main results. Section 3 describes the fluid–structure operator, followed by basic computations in Section 4. Sections 5 and 6 are devoted to the proof of Lq-maximal regularity and the establishment of uniform-in-time boundedness. We first address the L2 case in Section 5 by proving the self-adjointness of the fluid-structure operator (Lemma 5.1). For the general Lq case ( 1 < q < ∞), we derive crucial a priori estimates in Section 6 (Lemma 6.1) via a localization method that patches together known results for whole-space and bounded-domain problems. Using these estimates as a key tool, we establish time-dependent Lq-maximal regularity in Section 6.1. Sub-sequently, in Section 6.2, we improve these results for the range 1 < q < 3/2 by showing that the solution is uniformly bounded independent of time T . This uni-form estimate is achieved through a contradiction argument utilizing the previously derived a priori estimates and the uniqueness of the solution (Lemma 6.2). In Section 7, we establish the boundedness of the semigroup by exploiting the sectoriality of the fluid-structure operator, as maximal regularity alone does not di-rectly guarantee the uniform boundedness of the semigroup.
